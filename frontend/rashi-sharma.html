About

Rashi Sharma at a glance

Rashi Sharma finished her schooling in Pundri, completing Class 10 in 2023 and Class 12 in 2024, then completed the NIELIT O Level course where she learned HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Python, IoT and C++. She is now building on that foundation with a Digital Marketing with AI course, so her work combines clean front-end code with data-driven marketing.

What sets her work apart is the combination: she does not treat a website as only a design job or only a marketing job. She builds the product and then optimises how it is discovered and used, from page structure and speed to on-page SEO and analytics.

Technology is her canvas and marketing is her voice — she creates work people remember.
